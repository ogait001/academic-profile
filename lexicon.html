Lexicon of the Gaitan Topology

Twenty-three interconnected concepts, arranged not alphabetically but by ontological order — from the non-derivative ground through temporal operation, anthropological dynamics, and topological principle, to the cultural pathologies that follow from displacement. Each entry defines a structural term, relation, or operation within the framework rather than offering a general dictionary meaning.

The Relation field of each entry names the concepts with which it stands in a structurally significant relation. Those names are live—follow them to read the Lexicon as the network it describes.

Foundational Propositions

Five axiomatic commitments from which the entries above derive. They are not summaries of individual concepts but claims about the structure as a whole.

IEvery created actuality occurs only at the invariant Now.
IIBecoming proceeds through condensation constituted by inheritance and reception.
IIITime is the constitutive and irreversible order that results from the relation among condensed actualities.
IVEternal Presence sustains the Now without entering temporal succession.
VThe topology is lemniscatic because continuity and openness coexist at every crossing.
