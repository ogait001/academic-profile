Gaitan Topology · Research Framework

Key terms & structural propositions

A systematic reference for the ontological and topological concepts developed across the essays. Terms form the foundational layer; propositions are structural claims built from them.

Key terms — foundational layer
Type: Ontological Topological Structural Civilizational
1Now (ontological)
Ontological

The invariant point of actualization where every being becomes actual. It does not flow, does not move, and belongs to no one — the metaphysical condition simultaneously sustaining every event in the cosmos.

The crossing of the lemniscate: site of freedom, identity, and the reorientation of the will.

2Nonderivative
Structural

That whose being depends on nothing else in order to exist. It does not receive actualization — it gives it. The self-sustaining foundation of the Now: the I AM.

The only reality that does not participate in becoming but sustains it.

3Temporal density
Ontological

The ontological thickness of a moment, determined by the depth of the change that occurs within it. An act of reorientation of the will can be denser than years of superficial repetition.

Explains why human life is measured by crossings of the Now, not by duration.

4Structural inertia
Structural

The ontological resistance to change arising from the repetition of acts in the Now. Each decision leaves a trace that facilitates or hinders the next, generating a trajectory that may consolidate into orientation or deviation.

Explains habit, fall, fixation, and the increasing difficulty of reorientation without grace.

5Relativity of fullness
Topological

Fullness does not depend on the content of the gift but on the position from which it is received. At the center (the crossing point), gifts are inexhaustible; away from the center, they generate renewed thirst.

Allows hope, desire, and satisfaction to be reinterpreted through spiritual topology.

6Topological relativity
Topological

The variation of meaning, fullness, and orientation according to position within the lemniscatic structure. Not relativism — structural dependence. The same reality changes its effect depending on proximity to the center.

Explains why the same grace can be fullness, erosion, or rejection depending on the orientation of the will.

7Alpha and Omega
Ontological

Not two moments but the single eternal self-declaration of the foundation of being. Alpha precedes all becoming; Omega is the fullness toward which it is oriented. Both are simultaneous — they belong to the nonderivative order.

Names the foundation that sustains the Now and keeps the arc of becoming open.

8Compatibility with maintenance
Civilizational

The condition by which modern systems — technological, economic, cultural — prioritize persistence of forms through external support over genuine transformation through real constraint.

Produces the Uninhabited Now: a present filled with persistent yet hollow forms, enduring without crossing the lemniscatic threshold where identity is renewed.

Structural propositions — derived layer
P1 The Gaitan Topology ⌄

A structural model of temporality in which the lemniscate represents two loops — past and future — intersecting at a singular Now, the only point where actualization occurs. Time is not a flow but a continuous traversal through this invariant crossing point.

P2 The Now (crossing point) ⌄

The Now is a zero-thickness moment where potential becomes actual — the fixed locus of all events. Human agency, freedom, and grace operate exclusively here, within a microgap between possibility and actuality.

Draws on

Now (ontological) Temporal density Nonderivative
P3 Harmonic echo ⌄

Unrealized possibilities do not vanish but return at later moments in transformed form. Divine providence reconfigures lost opportunities — challenging linear views of time and fate.

Draws on

Now (ontological) Temporal density Alpha and Omega
P4 The Uninhabited Now ⌄

A condition in modern life where the present is filled with persistence — digital, biological, institutional — without genuine depth or transformation. Arises from artificial selection: systems that prioritize maintenance over real becoming.

Draws on

Now (ontological) Structural inertia Compatibility with maintenance Temporal density
P5 The Ghost Zone ⌄

A parallel interior topology where the self functions detached from reality — through rehearsed success or anticipated defeat. A state of displacement accelerated by algorithmic culture, rendering the crossing point functionally uninhabitable.

Draws on

Topological relativity Structural inertia Now (ontological)
P6 Alternate lemniscate ⌄

When the self abandons the true center, it constructs a false topology around a displaced center — pride, deferral, trauma, or illusion. These alternate lemniscates generate moral and existential collapse.

Draws on

Topological relativity Relativity of fullness Structural inertia Nonderivative
P7 Artificial selection ⌄

A critique of modern systems that replace natural transformation with engineered continuity. Unlike Darwinian selection — which reveals being through constraint — artificial selection simulates endurance without depth.

P8 Topological relativity (proposition) ⌄

Identical experiences yield opposite outcomes depending on their relation to the center. At the crossing point, reception is inexhaustible; off-center, every fulfillment generates renewed lack.
